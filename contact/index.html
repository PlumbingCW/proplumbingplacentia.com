What to say when you call

Start with the address and what is happening. Say whether water is moving, whether it is clean or sewage and whether you can reach the main shutoff. Those three answers decide what the plumber brings.

If you are not sure what is wrong, that is fine. Describe what you see, hear or smell. A hissing wall, a warm patch on the floor and a drain that gurgles all point to something specific, and the dispatcher works from there.

Have these ready if you can

None of this is required, but it speeds the visit up.

  • ✓Your street address and the nearest cross street. It helps the dispatcher plan the route and give a realistic arrival window.
  • ✓Which fixtures or rooms are affected. One sink and an entire house are different jobs.
  • ✓Whether the main water valve is off. If it is, tell the dispatcher. If it is stuck, tell them that too.
  • ✓The age of the water heater or pipes, if you know it. A tank near the end of its life changes the repair-or-replace answer.
  • ✓Any photos of the damage. They help your insurer, and they help the plumber.

Phone first for anything urgent

Email is slower than a call, so use it only when nothing is leaking. A message about a flooded laundry room can sit unread, while a call to the 24/7 line is answered around the clock.

For a planned job, such as a new water heater or a repipe, email works well because you can attach nothing but your questions. Include your address and a good time to call back, and ask for a call back.

Calls may be recorded

Calls to the line may be recorded for quality and training. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start. The privacy policy explains how call information is handled.

While you wait for the plumber

These steps limit damage until help arrives.

  • ✓Shut off the main water valve. It is usually near where the supply enters the house.
  • ✓Stop using fixtures. Do not flush or run taps until the plumber says it is safe.
  • ✓Keep people away from wet electrics. Switch the power off only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • ✓Photograph the damage. It helps with insurance and gives the plumber a head start.
  • ✓Smell gas? Leave first. Go outside, then call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 if there is danger.
