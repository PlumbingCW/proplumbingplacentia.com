What counts as a plumbing emergency in Yorba Linda

Some problems can wait for a morning appointment and some cannot. The ones below are treated as emergencies on the 24/7 line because water, waste or gas is already doing damage.

Call right away if you see any of these:

  1. A burst or split pipe. Water spraying from a wall, a ceiling or a crawl space needs the main valve closed and a plumber on the way.
  2. A sewer backup. Water rising in a tub or floor drain when you flush or run the washer means the main line is blocked.
  3. A failed water heater. A tank that is leaking onto the floor, or a unit that is hot to the touch and making noise, should be shut off.
  4. A gas smell. Leave the house first. Then call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place, and 911 if there is danger.

For a gas smell the call goes to the gas company, not a plumber. After the utility has made the house safe, a licensed plumber can look at the gas piping and appliances.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

The next ten minutes decide how much damage the water does. These steps are safe for most homeowners and make the plumber's work faster once we arrive.

Shut off the main water valve

Find the main shutoff where the water line enters the house and turn it fully closed. If you cannot find it, close the valve nearest to the leak.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, turn off power to that room, but only if the breaker panel is dry and you can reach it safely.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water. Keep children and pets out of the wet room.

Turn off the water heater

If the leak is at the heater, switch the gas control to off or the breaker to off, and close the cold water valve on top.

Photograph the damage

A few phone photos help with any insurance claim later. Take them before you start mopping.
