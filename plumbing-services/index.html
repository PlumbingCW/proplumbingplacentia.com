Choosing between repair and replacement

A good plumber does not push a bigger job than the problem needs. A dripping faucet is a cartridge. A tank heater that is leaking from the base is usually finished. A drain that clogs every month is telling you something about the pipe, and the answer might be a camera look, not another cable run.

When a repair and a replacement are both on the table, ask for both prices and the reasoning behind the difference. The water heater repair and water heater replacement pages walk through that choice, and the same logic applies to pipes and fixtures.

Permits, inspections and the city

Some plumbing work needs a city permit. The City of Placentia Building and Safety office at 401 Chapman Avenue, (714) 993-8124, lists water heaters, water lines, gas outlets, garbage disposals and pipe repair on its plumbing permit form. Ask the plumber who is pulling the permit before work begins.

California plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is Title 24, Part 5 and is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). State law also requires water heaters to be braced against earthquake movement, which is one reason a water heater installation is more than swapping a tank.
