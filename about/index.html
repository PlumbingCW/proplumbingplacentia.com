What we promise and what we do not

Plain promises are easier to keep than big ones, so this is the list.

  • ✓Licensed plumbers. The plumbers who come to your address are licensed, and you can check any California contractor license through the Contractors State License Board.
  • ✓Licensed and insured. Work is done under insurance, which protects your home if something goes wrong.
  • ✓A clear price before work starts. You hear the price and how it is built before anything begins.
  • ✓Same-day appointments when available. If the schedule allows it, the visit can happen today. If it does not, you hear that first.
  • ✓A 24/7 emergency line. Nights, weekends and holidays are answered the same way as a Tuesday morning.

What we do not claim

This site does not show customer reviews, star ratings, a count of years in business or a list of awards. Those numbers have to be real to mean anything, and a plumber you are about to let into your house should be judged on what you can check, not on a badge.

What you can check is the license, the price you are given and the explanation of the work. Ask for all three, and a good plumber will be glad to answer.

How the work is organized

Services are listed on the plumbing services page, grouped by problem. Urgent repairs sit beside planned work, so you can find the right page whether you are dealing with a flood or planning an upgrade.

Every page on this site is written by the Placentia Pro Plumbing team, and the guides under the blog are labeled the same way. The guides explain how plumbing works in Placentia homes so you can make better decisions before you pick up the phone.

What a typical visit looks like

No two jobs are the same, but most visits follow this path.

  • ✓The call. The dispatcher asks what is happening and gives you a price method and an arrival window.
  • ✓The arrival. The plumber checks the problem, usually starting with the fixture, the shutoff and the visible pipe.
  • ✓The explanation. You hear what failed and what the options are before the work begins.
  • ✓The repair and test. The fix is made and tested, and you are told what to watch for afterward.
  • ✓The follow-up. If a camera, permit or second visit is needed, it is explained before you leave.

Why local knowledge matters in Placentia

Placentia was incorporated in 1926 and became a charter city in 1965. Its population grew from about 5,000 in 1960 to about 25,000 in 1970, so a lot of the housing is old enough to have aging pipes. A plumber who knows that can ask better questions on the first call.

The city's sewer system is roughly 50 to 100 years old and its water comes from Golden State Water or Yorba Linda Water District depending on the street. Those details change what a repair involves, and the team writes the service pages with them in mind. The leak detection page is a good example. Every job is measured against the UPC-based California Plumbing Code.

Who we work for

Homeowners are the main audience, from older houses near Old Town to newer tracts across the city. Landlords and property managers call when a tenant has a backup, and small businesses call about drains, restrooms and water heaters.

The same line handles all of them. A restaurant floor drain gets the same clear price method as a kitchen sink, and the commercial plumbing page covers the business side.
