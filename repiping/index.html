Repiping in Placentia, CA

Repeated pinhole leaks and low pressure can mean the pipes are at the end of their life. A licensed plumber explains the options and the price first.

Repiping in Placentia, CA means replacing the supply pipes inside a home that have worn out, leaked repeatedly or lost pressure. It is a planned project, and the 24/7 line at (714) 750-8637 is also open for any leak that cannot wait. A licensed plumber looks at the pipes before anyone quotes a figure.

Repiping is not the same as repairing one leak or replacing the main line from the street. It covers the supply pipes inside the house. The water line repair page covers the service line from the meter to the house, which is a different job.

Signs the pipes inside the house are wearing out

One leak does not mean a house needs repiping. A pattern does. Pinhole leaks in different places, rusty or discolored water, low pressure that gets worse, and repairs that keep failing next to the last one are the usual signs.

When the pipe wall is thin everywhere, patching one spot only moves the failure down the line. The plumber checks the visible runs, the pressure and the age of the home, and tells you whether repair or replacement is the better use of money.

What a plumber looks at before recommending a repipe

  • ✓Pipe material. Copper, galvanized steel, PEX and other types fail in different ways, and the plumber identifies what the house has.
  • ✓Leak history. Several leaks in a short time point to pipe wear rather than bad luck.
  • ✓Water pressure and flow. Low flow at several fixtures can mean corrosion inside the pipe.
  • ✓Water color. Brown or orange water that clears after running may mean rust in the line.
  • ✓Hidden runs. A moving water meter with every fixture off suggests a leak in a run you cannot see. Leak detection confirms it.

What it is like to live through a repipe

Water is shut off during the work, usually for part of each day. Plan for bottled water, a place to shower and a way to cook. Ask the plumber how long the house will be without water on each day, and for the likely number of days.

Walls are opened where the new lines run, which is why many people schedule the repipe with other work. If a bathroom remodel is planned, a repipe can be done first and the walls left open. A new fixture also needs correct supply, which the plumbing installation page covers.

What to do before the repiping crew arrives

A little preparation keeps a whole-house job safe and quick.

  • ✓Find the main shutoff. Know where it is so water can be stopped at once if a joint lets go.
  • ✓Clear access. Move furniture and boxes from walls, closets and the water heater area where pipes run.
  • ✓Protect valuables. Cover or move items near the work, since drywall dust and open walls come with the job.
  • ✓Keep children and pets away. Open walls, tools and shut-off water make the work area unsafe for them.

Permits and inspections

Repiping is work the City of Placentia may need to review. The Building and Safety office at (714) 993-8124 publishes a permit form that lists water lines and pipe repair. New piping is installed to the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

  • 1Ask the city. Building and Safety can tell you whether your project needs a permit. Do not assume either way.
  • 2Ask the plumber. A licensed plumber explains how permits and inspections fit into the schedule.
  • 3Check the contractor. The California Contractors State License Board lists plumbing as a C36 license and offers an online license check.
  • 4Keep the paperwork. A record of the work helps at resale and with insurance.

What drives the price of a repipe

A clear price is given before work starts. These are the factors.

House size and layout

More fixtures, more floors and longer runs mean more pipe and more time.

Pipe material

Copper, PEX and other materials differ in cost and in how they are installed.

Wall access

A house with open framing is faster than one with finished walls and tile.

Drywall and finish work

Patching walls is a separate cost or handled by a separate trade.

Scope

A partial repipe costs less than a whole house, and the plumber explains the difference.

Repiping for age or wear is not usually covered. Sudden water damage from a failed pipe may be, so ask your agent and keep photos and the invoice.
