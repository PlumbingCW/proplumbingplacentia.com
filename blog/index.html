A note on do-it-yourself plumbing

Some jobs are reasonable for a careful homeowner, such as replacing a toilet wax ring or fixing a bathtub faucet drip. Others, such as gas lines, sewer repairs and water heater installation, carry real risk and may need a permit through the City of Placentia Building and Safety office.

The guides say where the line sits. If you are unsure, stop and call, because a mistake with a gas fitting or a main water line costs more than the visit.

How the guides are written

Every guide is bylined to the Placentia Pro Plumbing team and dated. Local facts come from public sources such as the City of Placentia and the water utilities. Where a number cannot be confirmed, the guide tells you to check your own bill or ask your utility instead of guessing.

Guides never show prices that cannot be backed up. Price factors are explained instead, so you know what moves a quote and can compare it fairly.
