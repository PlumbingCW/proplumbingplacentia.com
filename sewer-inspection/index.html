Sewer inspection in Placentia, CA uses a waterproof camera on a flexible cable to look inside your sewer line. Call (714) 750-8637 to book. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows, and a clear price is given before work starts.

A camera inspection shows roots, cracks, offset joints, sagging sections, scale and blockages. It also shows how far down the line the problem sits. That turns a guess into a plan and makes it easier to compare repair options.

What the camera looks for

The camera is pushed from a cleanout or an access point toward the street connection. The plumber watches a live screen as it travels and notes problems along the way.

Roots show up as fine hair or thick masses at joints. Cracks look like dark lines in the pipe. A belly is a low spot that holds water. An offset joint is a step where two pipe sections no longer line up. Scale and grease show as a rough, narrowed wall. The footage lets a plumber judge a pipe against the UPC-based California Plumbing Code, Title 24 Part 5, before any repair is quoted.

When a camera inspection makes sense

Not every drain problem needs a camera, but these situations are worth one.

  • ✓A drain that keeps clogging. If a cable clears it and it returns, the camera shows why.
  • ✓Before buying a home. The sewer line is out of sight. The footage shows whether it is a problem to be priced in.
  • ✓After a backup. The camera finds the cause so you can fix the pipe, not just clear it.
  • ✓Before a remodel or landscaping. Knowing where the line runs and how it looks avoids surprises.
  • ✓A sewage smell outdoors. The camera can show a break that is letting wastewater out.

Locating the line and measuring the distance

A camera head can carry a transmitter, so a locator above ground can mark where the pipe runs and how deep it sits. That matters when a spot repair is planned, because the dig has to be in the right place.

The plumber also records the distance from the cleanout to each problem. You can see the footage and ask questions, and the findings are used to explain the options if repair is needed.
